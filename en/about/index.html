About Me 🤔

Hello friend! Welcome to my small corner of the internet.
Before you ask, yes — I am serious, but I also like to play a little game with words.

My Philosophy 💡

Don’t you have some B number in your heart?

This is my life motto. In proper English, it might mean:
“Don’t you have a ballpark idea in your mind?”

Who Am I? 🐼

  • A code wrangler who writes Java and Go, and sometimes talks to computers more than people.
  • A full-stack explorer — backend, frontend, cloud — all mixed in my coffee.
  • A guy who feels uncomfortable if not tinkering.
  • Born curious, occasionally sarcastic, permanently caffeinated.

Things I Believe In 📜

  1. If code works, don’t touch… until it doesn’t.
  2. Documentation is like vegetables — good for you, but easy to forget.
  3. Deadlines are flexible, bugs are eternal.
  4. Always keep a B number in your heart — whatever that means to you.

Contact 📬

If you want to talk about tech, life, or just exchange some fun phrases,
you can find me in the code, between the commits, or maybe in the coffee shop.
